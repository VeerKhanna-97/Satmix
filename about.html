Our Mission

Automating Wealth for the Next Billion

Satmix was born out of a simple idea: investing shouldn't be a chore, and volatility shouldn't scare you away. By combining micro-savings with secure crypto baskets, we make building a portfolio effortless.

Our Story

Founded by Sheiden Borges, Veer Khanna, and Shashank Jajodia, Satmix was created to bridge the gap between traditional daily micro-savings (like gold savings apps) and the high-growth digital asset market. Combining Sheiden's 4+ years of blockchain expertise, Veer's design and full-stack development, and Shashank's financial strategy, we built a fully compliant micro-savings system for Indian Web3.

Our Mission

To make cryptocurrency accumulation safe, simple, and automated for the next billion users. We believe that financial freedom should be passive and anxiety-free. By automating daily micro-investments as low as ₹10, we make compounding accessible to retail savers without the need for constant market timing.

Our Vision

To build the most trusted, fully FIU-compliant Web3 wealth management platform in India. We aim to democratize access to diversified digital asset index baskets, helping retail savers passively beat inflation and secure their long-term milestones on autopilot.

Why Daily Micro-Savings?

In traditional finance, investing often requires large lump sums and constant market analysis. This leaves average retail savers behind. Taking inspiration from apps like Jar and Gullak that modernized gold savings, Satmix brings the power of automation and compounding to cryptocurrency.

By investing just ₹10 to ₹100 daily, you utilize Rupee Cost Averaging (RCA). This means you buy more assets when prices are low and fewer when they are high, smoothing out crypto's signature volatility without checking charts.

We operate in full compliance with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) of India, bringing banking-grade security and absolute peace of mind to Web3 digital assets.
